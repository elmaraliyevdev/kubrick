Product Documentation – Kubrick HR (AI-Powered Resume Scanner & Ranker)


This document provides a comprehensive overview of Kubrick HR, including vision, features, user flows, competitors, and monetization. It is formatted so you can copy directly into Confluence.

🚀 Product Overview

Kubrick HR is an AI-powered resume scanner and candidate ranking platform designed for recruiters, HR teams, and hiring managers. It automates the parsing, analysis, and ranking of resumes against job descriptions, enabling faster, smarter, and bias-reduced hiring decisions.

Vision:

To empower recruiters and companies with an AI-driven tool that turns resumes into actionable insights, saving time and improving quality of hire.

🎯 Target Users

Recruiters & HR professionals at small-to-mid-sized companies

Hiring managers looking for quick shortlists

Recruitment agencies managing multiple clients

🏢 Competitor Landscape

Jobscan – Resume-to-JD match tool (job seeker focus)

Skima AI – Enterprise-grade parsing & ranking with ATS integrations

RChilli / DaXtra / Textkernel – Resume parsing software

Rezi, Kickresume, Teal – Candidate-side resume optimization tools

Kubrick HR Differentiators:

Affordable & lean compared to enterprise ATS

Focus on recruiter-side ranking (not job seeker side)

Local language support (English, Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish)

Transparent scoring with clear explanations

⚙️ Core Features

1. Resume Parsing

Upload: PDF, DOCX, TXT (OCR support later)

Extract: Contact info, skills, experience, education, certifications

Structured profile in standardized format

2. Job Description Parsing

Paste JD or upload doc

Extract key requirements (skills, experience, keywords)

3. AI-Powered Matching & Ranking

Match % score between candidate & JD

Highlight matched, missing, and weak skills

Transparent reasoning for score

4. Recruiter Dashboard

Job management (create, edit, archive)

Candidate list ranked by match %

Color-coded scoring badges (Green, Yellow, Red)

Search and filter candidates

5. Candidate Detail View

Parsed profile summary

AI insights (skills matched/missing, gap analysis)

AI-generated candidate pitch

Export options (PDF/CSV)

6. Reporting & Exports

Job summary reports with top matches

Export lists (CSV, PDF, Shareable link)

7. Multi-language Support

MVP: English

Phase 2+: Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish

8. Integrations (Phase 2+)

ATS integration (Greenhouse, Lever, Zoho)

Slack/email notifications

Candidate sourcing (LinkedIn, GitHub)

🔄 Recruiter User Flows

1. Login / Onboarding

Recruiter logs in or creates account

Empty state shows CTA: “Create your first job”

2. Dashboard

View job list with candidate counts

CTA: [+ Create New Job]

3. Job Creation

Enter job title + JD

Save job → redirect to Job Detail

4. Candidate Upload & Ranking

Upload resumes (bulk drag & drop)

Progress bar for parsing

Ranked candidate list by match %

5. Candidate Detail

Parsed resume with insights

AI pitch + matched/missing skills

Export or shortlist candidate

6. Reports & Export

Job summary with top matches

Export CSV/PDF/share link

💰 Monetization

Pro ($99/mo): Up to 500 resumes + AI summaries

✅ Summary

Kubrick HR is a lean AI recruiting tool that gives recruiters instant insights into candidate-job fit. Unlike bloated ATS systems, Kubrick HR focuses on resume parsing, matching, and ranking — fast, affordable, and built for real recruiter workflows.
